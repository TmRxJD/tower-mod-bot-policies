Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: October 7, 2026

The bot processes Discord information to provide community moderation and event features. Some moderation features send selected messages, images, and moderation context to Groq for AI analysis, with a custom codec reducing exposure of personal identifiers in supported workflows. You can request access, modification, correction, or deletion by contacting tmrxjd or any moderator of the official The Tower Discord, or by opening a ticket in that server and stating your request.

1. Who operates the bot

The Tower Mod Bot (the “bot”) is operated and maintained by TmRxJD (Discord username tmrxjd; “we,” “us”) and used by Discord moderators to support community moderation. Moderators use the bot's tools and handle server moderation matters; TmRxJD is responsible for operating and maintaining the bot. This policy covers information processed by the bot and visits to this policy website. Discord and individual server administrators operate their own services and may have separate privacy practices.

2. Information we process

The information processed depends on enabled features, server configuration, and the permissions granted to the bot:

  • Discord identifiers and server information: user IDs, usernames or display names, server, channel, thread, message, and role IDs, membership and role information, and related timestamps.
  • Messages and interactions: message text, message links, reactions, commands, button selections, and form submissions used for moderation or community features. Relevant attachments, screenshots, image content, and attachment URLs or hashes may be processed for submitted content or image analysis.
  • Moderation records: warnings, reasons, evidence, moderator IDs, actions and outcomes, appeals and appeal text, watchlist entries and history, and watchlist thread transcripts. Moderation profiles can include activity summaries, conversation partners, commonly used channels, semantic summaries, and inferred risk, behavior, or contribution scores.
  • Account links: associations between Discord accounts recorded by moderators, including primary and alternate account IDs.
  • Community features: poll questions and votes, giveaway entries and winners, event submissions and results, and related user IDs. A poll displayed as anonymous can still store user IDs with votes internally; “anonymous” does not mean that no identifiable vote record exists.
  • Operational information: configuration, interaction state, diagnostic logs, errors, and information needed to operate and troubleshoot the bot.

Information comes from Discord's API and from users or moderators interacting with the bot. The bot accesses messages and other context available to it through Discord permissions. It does not require your Discord password or payment details. Do not submit passwords or sensitive personal information in commands, appeals, or attachments.

3. How we use information

We use information to enforce configured server rules, assist moderators, keep moderation history, handle appeals and account links, operate polls, giveaways and events, deliver requested community utilities, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot the service. AI-generated summaries and scores assist moderation and can be inaccurate. They are not statements of fact about a person; contact server moderators to contest a moderation decision.

We do not sell Discord API data or use it for advertising. We do not use Discord message content to train machine-learning or AI models.

4. AI analysis and service providers

The bot currently uses Groq for cloud AI moderation and image analysis. Depending on the feature, selected conversation context, message text, screenshots or other images, identifiers, moderation history, and profile summaries may be included in requests. Processing happens outside Discord on the provider's infrastructure. Provider handling and retention are governed by the applicable service agreement and provider policies; this policy does not promise zero retention by a provider.

Bot records are stored in local databases and files and, where configured, an Appwrite database service. Discord receives bot messages and interaction responses. Hosting, database, and AI providers process information as needed to deliver these services. We may disclose information when required by law. We do not make bot databases publicly available.

5. Who can see information

The operator and authorized maintainers can access operational records as needed to run the bot. Server moderators can receive moderation records, evidence, watchlist logs, and AI reports through the bot's configured workflows. Poll results, giveaway winners, event submissions, and other bot output may be visible to members who can access the relevant Discord channel. Channel access is controlled by server permissions; users should consider the visibility of the channel before submitting information.

6. Storage and retention

Moderation history, account links, watchlist history and transcripts, profiles, and community records are persistent records. Most do not currently have a fixed automatic deletion period. We retain them only while needed for the bot's stated functionality and handle deletion requests as described below. Removing the bot from a server or leaving a server does not itself automatically erase existing stored records.

Evaluation report sessions currently expire after seven days by default; expired sessions are removed when the session store is next accessed or pruned. Operational logs and local transcript backups can persist until removed through operational maintenance or a deletion request. Deletion requests cover bot-controlled database copies, local files, and backups containing the requested data. We delete API data promptly when requested by the applicable user or Discord, when it is no longer necessary for the bot's functionality, or when the bot stops operating, except where retention is required by applicable law.

7. Access, modification, correction, and deletion requests

You can make a privacy request by contacting tmrxjd or any moderator of the official The Tower Discord. You can also open a ticket in the official The Tower Discord and request that your bot-held data be accessed, modified, corrected, or deleted. State that the request concerns The Tower Mod Bot, include your Discord user ID and relevant server ID, and describe the data and change you are requesting. Moderators can receive your request and coordinate with TmRxJD where operator access is needed. Use a private ticket or private contact rather than posting personal information publicly.

We may ask you to verify control of the account before disclosing or changing records. We will respond and process verified requests promptly and within applicable legal deadlines. We will explain any retention required by law. We cannot delete records independently controlled by Discord or other people, such as messages copied by a server moderator; requests concerning those copies should also go to Discord or the relevant server administrator. You may also ask about privacy rights available under the laws where you live.

8. Security and the custom privacy codec

The bot uses a custom codec to help protect personally identifiable information (PII) in supported AI moderation workflows. It replaces Discord participant IDs and mentions with aliases, replaces message references with short labels, and replaces channel mentions and URLs with generic markers in encoded message text. The bot retains the association between aliases and the original Discord records so it can interpret AI results and present relevant information to authorized moderators.

This reduces exposure of direct identifiers to the AI service. The codec is pseudonymization and data minimization, not encryption or a guarantee of complete anonymity. Relevant message text, screenshots, and other submitted content can still contain personal information, and not every feature uses the same encoding. The bot's underlying moderation records remain identifiable for its stated functions.

Access to bot-controlled storage and service credentials is restricted to authorized operators. No system can guarantee complete security. Report suspected unauthorized access to tmrxjd or any moderator of the official The Tower Discord, including through a private ticket. We will investigate incidents and notify affected users and Discord as required.

9. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It includes no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies, or contact forms. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site under its privacy statement.

10. Age requirements and changes

The bot is intended for people eligible to use Discord under its minimum age rules and applicable local law. Contact us if you believe information about an ineligible child has been processed. We may update this policy as features or practices change. The current version and update date will remain available here; material changes will be communicated through the bot or affected servers where appropriate.
